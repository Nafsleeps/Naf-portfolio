A little about me

I've called [CITY] home for most of my life, and I still get a lump in my throat handing over keys.

Before real estate I worked in a job where listening mattered more than talking, and that's stayed with me. Selling a home is rarely just about the property. It's about what comes next for your family, and getting that right takes patience and plain, honest advice.

I keep my client list small on purpose, so every vendor gets me, not a junior. You'll hear from me every week, whether the news is good or not, and I'll never push you towards a price or a timeframe that doesn't suit you.

If you're even thinking about selling, I'd love to have a cuppa and talk it through. No pressure, no obligation.
