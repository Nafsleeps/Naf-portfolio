01  The story

Nobody was saving me a seat.

I moved countries with a rough plan and a lot of nerve. That's it. That was the whole strategy.

Turns out it's most of what you need. You land somewhere new and the room is already full. No one hands you a chair. So you build one, sit down, and get to work before anyone notices you weren't invited.

That taught me the only thing worth learning early: start before you feel ready. Ship it rough. Fix it live. The gap between the people who talk about it and the people who have it running isn't talent — it's the first ugly version nobody wanted to make.

Somewhere in the middle of all that, AI stopped being a headline and became the tool. Not the hype — the leverage. One person with the right systems now moves like a team of ten. I've been obsessed with that edge ever since, and I build with it every single day.

So I start things. Ventures, sites, systems, agents. Some land. Some don't. The ones that land, land because I kept showing up long after it stopped being exciting.
