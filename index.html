Story

I came over from Bangladesh in 2023 to study. Pretty quickly I needed money — properly needed it — and the obvious move was to pick up casual shifts somewhere. I taught myself to build websites instead. The first ones were for restaurants. They took me forever and they were rough, but I finished them.

Then I walked into a phone repair shop I had never been in before and told them their website wasn't doing them any favours, and that I could fix it. They said yes. That was the first time anyone paid me for this.

There are a lot of businesses around Perth with no website at all, or one from ten years ago that nobody has touched since. I still work on my own, I've built 30 of them now and I stay on with every client month to month, and I can usually have something good up in about a week. That's the job.
